Demonstrates CCCL/libcu++ random-number facilities. 

Uses cuda::pcg64 and cuda::std::philox4x32 engines together with uniform, normal, poisson, and bernoulli distributions. 

Host-side template statistics helpers compute empirical mean and variance and compare against theoretical expectations.
